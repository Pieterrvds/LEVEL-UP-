Level 01 · The platform

Hey there!

Welcome to the LEVEL-UP movement. LEVEL-UP is where players and personal trainers meet. Pick a coach from our team, book a one-hour session straight from their weekly schedule and train one-on-one.

Every session you book earns XP and levels up your player profile. Our trainers level up too: the more players they coach, the higher their level. You push each other, rep after rep.

Want to go further? Check your body stats, log your own workouts and grab your gear in the item shop.
