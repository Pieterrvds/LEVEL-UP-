Level 01 · The platform

Hey there!

Welcome to the LEVEL-UP movement. LEVEL-UP is where players and personal trainers meet. Create your player, pick a coach from our team and book a one-hour session straight from their weekly schedule.

Your training becomes a game. Every session, workout and weekly check-in earns XP, and you climb from Rookie to Legend. Our trainers level up too: the more players they coach, the higher their level.

  1. 1

    Create your player

    Sign up and set your character stats: BMI, daily calories and your goal, tracked in your profile.

  2. 2

    Choose your trainer

    Meet the team and find the coach that fits your goal.

  3. 3

    Book a slot

    Pick an open hour in the weekly schedule. Your trainer gets notified straight away.

  4. ★

    Level up together

    Earn XP for every session and check-in. Your trainer earns XP for every player they coach.
